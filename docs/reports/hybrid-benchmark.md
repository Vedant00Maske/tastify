## 1. Protocol

| Period | Window (UTC) | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ranker-train | 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-10 | Platt calibration; LambdaRank training (labels: what users played next) |
| validation | 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-13 | CF configuration, hybrid weights, LambdaRank early stopping, MMR lambda, default exploration mode |
| test | 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-16 | reported once; plus three one-day rolling windows |

Every period is predicted from interactions before its start: signals, popularity and the collaborative models are retrained per period, and the content model uses catalogue metadata only. A user is evaluated when they have at least 5 distinct earlier tracks and at least one new track in the period; relevant items are those new tracks (already heard tracks are never recommended). Lists follow the served rules: at most 2 tracks per artist and one version per song, unless stated.

Pre-declared rules: the served ranker is the one with the higher validation NDCG@10; MMR lambda is the most diverse setting within 5% of the validation NDCG@10 at lambda = 1; the default exploration mode is `auto` unless `balanced` scores higher. Validation users: 5,000.

## 14. Limitations

- Two weeks of research listening only: no TASTIFY users yet, and no skips, likes or saves in the data. Their weights (spec §8.5), the negative-artist rules and the negative-artist penalty are configured, tested, but not tuned; they need real usage (Milestone 8).
- The rankers learn to predict the next days' listening of ListenBrainz users, who skew towards a few genres; the segments show where the models are weaker.
- Offline retrieval is exact; the API uses HNSW indexes (measured above). The API also bounds per-request work: item-item scoring reads the user's 300 strongest tracks and ALS fold-in their 1,000 strongest (the evaluation uses all of them).
- The served hybrid is non-commercial because of MusicBrainz genre tags; the CC0 variant quantifies the cost of dropping them.
- The user endpoints have no authentication until Milestone 4 and are served only in local and test environments.
