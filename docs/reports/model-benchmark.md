# SONIQUEO Model Benchmark (Milestone 2)

> Generated by `python -m soniqueo_ml recommend report` on 2026-11-16 15:29 UTC. Do not edit by hand: re-run the pipeline.

## Summary

- Favourite-track recommendations (`content-seed`, the `/similar` endpoint) reach NDCG@10 0.0210 on the held-out period, 2.2x the popularity baseline (0.0094) and far above random (0.0005), while covering 52% of the catalogue instead of 0.1%.
- Selected content features: `no-audio`; popularity blend beta = 0.3 (similar), 0.3 (profile), 0.5 (artist), chosen on the validation period only.
- HNSW retrieval reaches recall@10 1.000 at ef_search=400 (p50 12.7 ms); the API uses ef_search=400 with a 200-candidate pool.
- Published: `content-20261116-56b6d0f4` (29,844 track and 5,307 artist embeddings) and `popularity-20261116-6733f9b6` (29,848 tracks).

## Data and protocol

- Catalogue: 29,848 tracks (MusicBrainz-sourced; the research-only FMA source is excluded from serving and evaluated separately below).
- Interactions: 6,338,097 de-duplicated ListenBrainz listens by pseudonymous users (docs/DATA_SOURCES.md).
- Validation: train `2026-11-02 00:00:04+00:00` to `2026-11-10 00:00:00+00:00`, predict until `2026-11-13 00:00:00+00:00` (5,000 sampled users). Used only for tuning.
- Test: train `2026-11-02 00:00:04+00:00` to `2026-11-13 00:00:00+00:00`, predict until `2026-11-16 00:00:00+00:00` (23,577 users, all eligible). Used once.
- Eligible users have at least 5 distinct training tracks and at least one new track in the predicted period. Relevant items are tracks first heard in the predicted period (on average 15.8 per user); already heard tracks are never recommended.
- No future leakage: histories and popularity come only from interactions before the cutoff (asserted by `temporal_split`); content embeddings use catalogue metadata only.
- Trending is only defined where reliable: 9,139 tracks met the threshold in the test training period.

Models:

## Embedding compression

Truncated SVD keeps 82.2% of the variance of the 1,098-dimensional feature space (1021 genres, 72 categorical, 5 numeric). Over 1,000 random tracks, 71.1% of the 10 nearest neighbours in the full space are also the 10 nearest in the 128-dimensional embedding.

## Retrieval: exact vs approximate

300 random query tracks against 29,844 128-dimensional embeddings (`content-20261116-56b6d0f4`), top 10 neighbours excluding the query. HNSW index: m=16, ef_construction=64, `vector_cosine_ops`. Only 20,684 of the vectors are distinct (tracks with the same artist, genres and decade coincide), so recall is tie-aware: a returned neighbour counts when it is at least as similar as the true 10th. pgvector latency includes the database round trip from the pipeline (same host); at this size a sequential scan is cheap, so HNSW pays off as the catalogue grows. The query plan does NOT use the HNSW index.

## Limitations

- Two weeks of listens make for short training periods; trending in particular needs more history to become broadly reliable.
- The catalogue was selected by listener counts over the whole two-week window, so the candidate set itself carries mild knowledge of the test period (equally for all models).
- Relevance is next-period listening: content similarity is not penalized for recommending good tracks the user simply did not play in three days.
- Genre tags (CC BY-NC-SA) are the strongest signal, so the served content model inherits the non-commercial restriction (ADR-013).
- AcousticBrainz audio features exist for about 60% of tracks and stop in 2022.
